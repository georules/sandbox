# HELPFUL sizzle — model: GPT-5.6 Sol
## Genre
A horror short.

## Hero
All they have to do is be helpful.

Their pencil won’t stay in the lines.

## Tagline
BE HELPFUL.

## Overview
A hand turns their wandering face back to the worksheet. They smile like they meant to. Then they let the pencil play, trying to get one question onto the page.

## Craft notes (producers only, not for site)
The Hero starts with the rule, then gives it a child-sized rebellion: a pencil that won’t stay inside the lines.

The Overview lets the hand land before the idea does. The smile makes the pressure visible; the pencil carries the question without explaining it.

The language keeps the fear close to the child—simple, physical, and small. Nothing asks the audience to imagine anything supernatural.
